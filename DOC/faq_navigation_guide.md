# FAQ Navigation Guide — CSE Fest 2026

This guide explains how to access, view, and manage the FAQ section of the platform.

---

## 1. Public View (Participants & Visitors)

The public FAQ section is embedded directly on the landing page for easy access:

1. **Navigation**: Open a web browser and navigate to the platform homepage: `https://csefest.smuct.edu.bd/` (or `http://localhost:3000` in local development).
2. **Scroll to Section**: Scroll down to the bottom of the homepage. You will find the accordion FAQ cards showing commonly asked questions (such as eligibility, registration rules, and deadlines).
3. **Usage**: Click on any question card. It will smoothly expand to show the detailed answer.

---

## 2. Administrative Management (Organizers & Admins)

To add, edit, remove, or reorder FAQs, administrators must use the CMS Dashboard:

1. **Prerequisite**: Log in with an account that has the `admin` role.
2. **Access Admin Panel**: Go to the Admin Dashboard at: `/admin/dashboard`.
3. **Enter CMS Management**:
   - In the sidebar menu, click on the **CMS** link.
   - Alternatively, navigate directly to: `https://csefest.smuct.edu.bd/admin/cms` (or `http://localhost:3000/admin/cms` locally).
4. **Select FAQs Tab**:
   - The CMS page contains four tabs: *Announcements*, *Ticker*, *FAQs*, and *Contact*.
   - Click on the **FAQs** tab.
5. **Manage FAQs**:
   - **Create**: Click the **Add FAQ** button, fill in the Question and Answer fields, specify a display order, and click Save.
   - **Edit/Hide**: Click **Edit** on any FAQ card in the list to update its text or toggle its public visibility.
   - **Reorder**: Use the **Up/Down arrows** on the right of any FAQ item to change its display order instantly.
   - **Delete**: Click the trash icon to permanently remove an FAQ.
